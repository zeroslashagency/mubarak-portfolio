# About v2: /me, a click-through film

A new prototype page at `/me` that plays Mubarak's story like a short film. A visitor clicks "Watch my story" in the home about section. The screen goes black with letterbox bars, then each click moves to the next scene: a chapter title card, a full-bleed photo with a slow zoom, and one subtitle line that types in character by character. Seven chapters run from childhood in Chennai to the ML work today, and the film ends on credits with contact and project links. The current minimal about section and `/about` page stay unchanged; `/me` is a parallel version to try out.

The canvas above shows the entry button, the cold open, the three beats of one scene, the credits and the mobile layout. The storyline below is a draft. Lines marked *[needs your memory]* cannot be written without you, and the questions at the bottom gather them.

## Objective

Done means: `/me` exists in the static export, plays all seven chapters by click, tap or arrow keys, works on mobile and desktop, respects reduced motion, and every line in the film is true and in Mubarak's own words. No invented achievements or numbers.

Each chapter gets one or two short lines, at most about 120 characters each. Short lines keep the typing effect readable and the whole film around 60 to 90 seconds of clicking. Chapter 01 is the emotional hook, so it needs a specific, real memory; a generic "I was always curious" line would sound like every other portfolio.

</Callout>

## How it plays

- **Click or tap, Space, right arrow:** if the line is still typing, finish it; otherwise go to the next beat.
- **Left arrow or swipe right:** previous scene. **Esc:** back to home.
- **Beats per chapter:** title card (fades in, holds about 1.5 s) → scene (photo with slow zoom, line types at about 35 ms per character) → dip to black.
- **Progress:** a small `01 / 07` counter bottom left, "Click for next" bottom right.
- **Reduced motion:** no zoom and no typing; lines appear whole and scenes crossfade.
- **Sound:** off by default. Optional soft ambient track and key tick, toggled from the bottom bar (see Open Questions).
